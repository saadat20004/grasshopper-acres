The stay

Everything is set up. Just bring your people.

No pitching tents, no hauling gear. Arrive to a ready camp on the quiet six acre side of the farm, with open pasture in front and woods behind.

"A complete exhale, and exactly what I needed."Natalie G., June 2026

A roomy four season bell tent

A 6 meter canvas tent with sturdy cots and bedding provided. The indoor wood stove keeps it warm and cozy on cool nights, so it works in every season.

Fire pits and an outdoor kitchen

Two fire pits, a BBQ grill and a cooking area for full meals. Pots, pans, utensils and dishes are all there, plus firewood at a wood station near camp.

A private, spotless outhouse

Your own wooden outhouse with a composting toilet and a foot pump sink. Guests keep calling it the cleanest camp bathroom they have seen.

Open land and starry nights

Flat, wide pasture with little light pollution. 300 feet of soft string lights frame camp at night, and deer, owls and birds visit from the woods.
